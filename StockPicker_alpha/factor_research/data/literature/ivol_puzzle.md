# Idiosyncratic Volatility Puzzle (Ang et al.)

**Doc id:** `ivol_puzzle.md`

Ang, A., Hodrick, R. J., Xing, Y., & Zhang, X. (2006). The Cross-Section of Volatility
and Expected Returns. *Journal of Finance*.

- Stocks with high idiosyncratic volatility have historically earned **lower** average returns
  (the ivol puzzle) — the long-short sign is intentional when shorting high-ivol names.
- Results are sensitive to microstructure, universe filters, and weighting schemes.
- Monitor liquidity, short-sale constraints, and whether the puzzle survives FF4 and costs.

For governance: flag suspiciously strong Sharpes; confirm the economic story matches the implemented sign.

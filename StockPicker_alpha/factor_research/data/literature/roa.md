# Profitability / ROA (Novy-Marx)

**Doc id:** `roa.md`

Novy-Marx, R. (2013). The Other Side of Value: The Gross Profitability Premium.
*Journal of Financial Economics*.

- Profitability / quality-style signals (including ROA variants) can complement value.
- Overlap with book-to-market is common; a composite may dilute the strongest single leg.
- Check whether the signal is incremental after controlling for value and investment.

For governance: document economic hypothesis, turnover, and collinearity with BtM before recommending a blend.

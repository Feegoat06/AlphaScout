# Momentum (Jegadeesh & Titman)

**Doc id:** `momentum.md`

Jegadeesh, N., & Titman, S. (1993). Returns to Buying Winners and Selling Losers:
Implications for Stock Market Efficiency. *Journal of Finance*.

- Intermediate-horizon relative strength (roughly 3–12 month formation) historically earns a premium.
- Momentum is well documented but **crash-prone**, especially around market rebounds after drawdowns.
- Practical monitors: regime stability, turnover, and capacity; do not treat full-sample Sharpe alone as conclusive.

For governance: keep sign and economic story attached; stress-test crash months before allocating capital.

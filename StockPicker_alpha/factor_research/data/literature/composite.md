# Composite Signal Blends

**Doc id:** `composite.md`

Equal-weight (or similar) composites of momentum, value, profitability, and volatility signals
can diversify noise across anomalies but often **dilute** the strongest single factor.

- Academic practice usually reports each anomaly separately before blending.
- Composites raise questions about overlapping exposures, turnover stacking, and unstable weights.
- Prefer documenting each leg's literature prior, then test whether the blend improves risk-adjusted
  performance after costs and regime splits.

For governance: do not auto-recommend composite over a cleaner single factor without incremental evidence.
